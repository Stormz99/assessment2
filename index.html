FAQs

How does URL shortening work?

URL shortening works by taking a long URL and creating a shorter, condensed version that redirects to the original URL. When a user clicks on the shortened link, they are redirected to the intended destination.

Is it necessary to create an account to use the URL shortening service?

No account is needed to shorten a link. Creating one lets you manage your links, customize them and see analytics.

Are the shortened links permanent? Will they expire?

Your shortened links stay active, and you can edit or remove them from your dashboard at any time.

Are there any limitations on the number of URLs I can shorten?

The Basic plan includes unlimited URL shortening. Paid plans add more features on top of that.

Can I customize the shortened URLs to reflect my brand or content?

Yes. You can create custom slugs and use branded domains so your links match your brand.

Can I track the performance of my shortened URLs?

Yes. Link analytics show you how your shortened URLs and QR codes are performing.

How secure is the URL shortening service? Are the shortened links protected against spam or malicious activity?

Links are checked to help protect against spam and malicious activity, and paid plans add extra security options.

What is a QR code and what can it do?

A QR code is a scannable image that takes people straight to your link when they point their phone camera at it.

Is there an API available for integrating the URL shortening service into my own applications or websites?

Yes. API access comes with the Teams plan, so you can connect Scissor to your own apps and websites.
